## Email us

**hello@amantrika.com**

That address reaches a person, not a queue. We answer within a working day, usually sooner.

Useful things to include, if they apply:

- The link to your invitation, or the slug you chose
- The phone or browser you were using when something went wrong
- A screenshot, if it is a display problem

## If your wedding is this week

Say so in the subject line. Anything with a date attached goes to the front.

## Wedding planners, printers and photographers

If you build invitations for your own clients, there is a partner arrangement — wholesale pricing, multiple invitations under one account, and the ability to hand an invitation over to the couple so they manage their own RSVPs. Email us with a line about your business and the city you work in.

## Press and partnerships

Same address. Please put "Press" or "Partnership" in the subject.

<Callout type="note" title="On the contact form">
There is no form on this page yet. We would rather send you to an address that definitely reaches us than to a form that might not — the previous version of this site had a contact form that rendered perfectly and submitted nowhere, and we are not repeating that.
</Callout>
